About WIMS

Women in Materials Science (WIMS) is a student organization at Texas A&M University. WIMS is open to anyone interested in materials science — any major, any year, no experience required. We host bi-weekly meetings, socials, and outreach events, and connect members with mentors, faculty, and industry professionals.
